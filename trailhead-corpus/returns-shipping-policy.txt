RETURNS & SHIPPING POLICY

OVERVIEW & CONTACT

This document sets out the returns, exchanges, and shipping policy for Trailhead Supply Co., an independent outdoor-gear shop founded in 2014. We have built this shop around a simple idea: Repair, don't replace. We want the gear you buy from us to last for years on the trail, and when something does go wrong, we want the path back to us to be clear and fair.

This policy covers all purchases made in our store at 214 Pine Street, Pinedale, WY 82941, as well as orders placed through our website at trailheadsupply.co. It explains how returns work, the difference between a refund and an exchange, which items cannot come back, how we handle damaged or defective gear, and how we ship your orders across the country.

If at any point you are unsure how this policy applies to your order, reach out before you mail anything back. We would rather answer a quick question than have a package go astray. You can contact our support team in three ways:

By phone at (307) 367-4180 during regular store hours.

By email at support@trailheadsupply.co, where we aim to reply within one business day.

In person at the counter at 214 Pine Street in Pinedale, Wyoming.

Throughout this document, "business days" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays. Where a window is measured in days, the clock starts on the date you receive your item, not the date you placed the order.

SECTION 1 — RETURNS

You have a 30-day full-refund window on most items purchased from Trailhead Supply Co. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may return it within 30 days of receiving it for a full refund of the purchase price.

To qualify for a full refund, items must be returned unused, in resalable condition, with all original tags still attached and in the original packaging. Gear that shows signs of use, soiling, or trail wear does not qualify for a refund under this section — see Section 3 for non-returnable items.

There are two ways to return an item.

In store: Bring the item, in its original packaging with tags attached, to the counter at 214 Pine Street, Pinedale, WY 82941. Please bring your receipt or order confirmation so we can locate your purchase.

By mail: Contact support@trailheadsupply.co or call (307) 367-4180 to request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number before shipping anything back. We cannot process mailed returns that arrive without an RMA number, so please wait for it before you send your package. Write the RMA number clearly on the outside of the box.

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. Refunds post within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take a few additional days for the credit to appear on your statement.

A note on boots. Boots are a special case, and we treat them with a little extra care because fit matters so much on the trail. You may return boots within 30 days if they have not been worn outdoors. Trying boots on indoors — walking around the house, testing the fit on carpet, checking how they feel with your hiking socks — is completely fine and will not void your return. We expect you to try them on.

What disqualifies a boot return is outdoor wear. Once a boot has been worn outside, the sole picks up dirt, grit, and scuffs that make the boot impossible to resell as new. So please do your fit testing indoors first. If the boots feel right indoors, take them outside with confidence. If they do not, send them back to us clean and unworn within the 30-day window.

This indoor-try-on allowance applies to footwear specifically. For all other categories, the standard unused-with-tags requirement applies.

If you are returning by mail, pack boots back in their original box and place that box inside a shipping carton. Shipping boots in their retail box alone, with a label slapped directly on the box, often results in a crushed and damaged box that we can no longer use. Protecting the original packaging protects your refund.

When in doubt about whether an item still qualifies, email a photo to support@trailheadsupply.co before you ship. We are happy to take a look and tell you what to expect.

SECTION 2 — EXCHANGES & STORE CREDIT

We offer a longer window for exchanges and store credit than we do for refunds, because we would always rather help you find the right piece of gear than simply send your money back.

Here are the two windows side by side, stated plainly so there is no confusion:

Refund window: 30 days. A return for a refund to your original payment method must happen within 30 days of receiving your item.

Exchange and store-credit window: 45 days. If instead of a refund you would like to exchange the item for a different size, color, or product, or take the value as store credit, you have 45 days from the date you received your item.

The difference matters. The 30-day clock is for getting your money back. The 45-day clock — fifteen days longer — is for swapping gear or banking the value with us for a future purchase. If you are past 30 days but still inside 45 days, a refund is no longer available, but an exchange or store credit still is.

The same condition rules apply to exchanges and store credit as to refunds: items must be unused, with original tags and packaging, and boots must not have been worn outdoors.

For an exchange, if the new item costs more than the original, you pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference is issued as store credit. Store credit never expires and can be used in store or online. To start an exchange, visit us at 214 Pine Street or email support@trailheadsupply.co for an RMA.

SECTION 3 — NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS

Some items cannot be returned, exchanged, or credited under any circumstances except where they arrive damaged or defective (see Section 4). Please review this list before you purchase, especially for final-sale gear.

Clearance and final-sale items. Any item marked as clearance or final sale at the time of purchase is sold as-is. These items are not eligible for a refund, exchange, or store credit. The discount reflects the fact that the sale is final. If an item is on the clearance rack or labeled final sale online, please be confident in your choice before buying.

Used or visibly worn gear. Gear that has been used outdoors, washed, soiled, or otherwise shows signs of wear cannot be returned. This includes boots worn outdoors, packs that have been loaded and carried on the trail, tents that have been pitched outside, and apparel that has been worn beyond an indoor fit check. We cannot resell used gear as new, and our policy is built around keeping returned items in resalable condition.

Gift cards. Gift cards are non-returnable and non-refundable. They do not expire, and they can be used toward any purchase in store or online, but they cannot be redeemed for cash or returned for a refund.

This list is not meant to discourage returns — most of what we sell is fully returnable. It simply marks the few categories where, by their nature, a return is not possible. If you are unsure whether an item falls into one of these categories, ask us before you buy.

SECTION 4 — DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS

We stand behind the gear we sell. If your item arrives damaged, or if you discover a manufacturing defect, we want to make it right quickly.

If your order arrives damaged in transit, or you find a defect when you first use the gear, contact support within 30 days of receiving the item. Email support@trailheadsupply.co or call (307) 367-4180. Please include your order number and clear photos of the problem — a cracked buckle, a failed seam, a leaking zipper, a sole that separated. Photos let us diagnose the issue fast and get a replacement or refund moving without a lot of back and forth.

For a confirmed defect or shipping damage reported inside that 30-day window, we will cover the cost of return shipping and send a replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer. You will not pay return shipping when the fault is ours or the manufacturer's.

Beyond the return window, defects are handled through the manufacturer's warranty. Many of the brands we carry — for packs, tents, footwear, and technical apparel — offer multi-year or lifetime warranties against defects in materials and workmanship. If a defect surfaces after our 30-day window has closed, contact us anyway. We will help you identify the correct manufacturer, point you to their warranty process, and hand off the claim so you are not navigating it alone. This warranty-handoff service is part of our Repair, don't replace ethos: a defect should be fixed, not a reason to throw good gear away.

A few practical notes on damaged and defective claims.

Keep your packaging until you are confident in your gear. If something arrives broken, the original box and any protective materials help us and the carrier assess what happened. This is especially true for carrier-damage claims, where the carrier may ask to inspect the packaging.

Document the problem early. The sooner you photograph an issue, the easier the claim. A photo taken the day the package arrives is far more useful than one taken three weeks later.

Normal wear is not a defect. Gear used hard on the trail will eventually show wear — that is expected and is not covered as a defect. A worn-down sole after a season of hiking, abrasion on a pack that has scraped against rock, or fading from sun exposure are signs of use, not manufacturing faults. Warranty coverage is for defects in materials and workmanship, not for the ordinary aging of well-used equipment.

When in doubt, ask. If you are not sure whether what you are seeing is a defect or normal wear, send us a photo. We have handled a lot of gear over the years, and we are glad to give you an honest read before you start a claim.

Our goal in every damaged-or-defective case is the same: get you back on the trail with working gear as fast as we reasonably can, and keep usable equipment out of the landfill wherever a repair or warranty claim can do the job.

SECTION 5 — SHIPPING & PROCESSING

Once you place an order at trailheadsupply.co, we get to work packing it. Orders are processed in 1 to 2 business days. Processing means we pick your items, inspect them, pack them carefully, and hand them to the carrier. Orders placed on weekends or holidays begin processing on the next business day.

We ship using two carriers: USPS and UPS. The carrier for a given order depends on the package size, weight, and destination — we choose whichever option gets your gear to you reliably and at the rate listed in Section 6. We do not currently offer a choice of carrier at checkout, but you are welcome to note a preference in the order comments and we will accommodate it when we can.

As soon as your order ships, we email you a tracking number. The tracking email goes to the address on your order, so please make sure your email address is entered correctly at checkout. If you do not see a tracking email within three business days of placing your order, check your spam folder first, then contact support@trailheadsupply.co and we will resend it.

Please double-check your shipping address before you submit your order. Carriers charge us to reroute or return misaddressed packages, and an incorrect address is the most common cause of a delayed delivery. If you notice a mistake in your address right after ordering, contact us immediately — if the order has not yet shipped, we can usually correct it.

SECTION 6 — SHIPPING ZONES & RATES

We keep our shipping rates simple and flat by zone, so you know what you will pay before you reach checkout. Zones are based on the destination state.

Zone 1 — within Wyoming. A flat rate of $6 on all orders shipped to a Wyoming address. As a Pinedale shop, we love sending gear to our neighbors across the state.

Zone 2 — the Mountain West. A flat rate of $9 on orders shipped to Montana, Idaho, Utah, or Colorado. These are the states right around us, and we batch shipments to keep the rate low.

Zone 3 — the rest of the contiguous United States. A flat rate of $12 on orders shipped anywhere else in the lower 48 states not covered by Zone 1 or Zone 2.

Alaska and Hawaii. Orders shipped to Alaska or Hawaii are charged the Zone 3 rate of $12 plus a $15 surcharge, for a total of $27. The surcharge reflects the higher cost carriers charge us to reach these destinations.

Free standard shipping over $75. Any order with a merchandise subtotal over $75, shipping anywhere in the contiguous United States, ships free via standard shipping. This applies to any zone in the lower 48 — Zone 1, Zone 2, or Zone 3. The free-shipping threshold is calculated on the merchandise subtotal before tax. Orders to Alaska and Hawaii do not qualify for free shipping because of the surcharge, though the surcharge structure above still applies.

A few clarifications to help you understand which rate applies to your order.

The zone is set by the destination, not the origin. Every order ships from our store in Pinedale, Wyoming, but the rate you pay depends on where the package is going. A package to Cheyenne pays the Zone 1 rate; a package to Denver pays the Zone 2 rate; a package to Chicago pays the Zone 3 rate.

Rates are flat within a zone, regardless of how many items you order, up to standard package sizes. If you place an unusually large or heavy order — say, multiple tents or a bulk apparel order — we may contact you about an adjusted shipping cost before we ship. We will never charge more than the quoted flat rate without first reaching out and getting your okay.

The free-shipping threshold looks at your merchandise subtotal. Discounts and promotions apply before the threshold is checked, so if a coupon brings your subtotal at or below $75, standard shipping rates apply. Taxes and any surcharges are not counted toward the $75.

Standard shipping is the only service level we offer at this time. We do not currently provide expedited, overnight, or guaranteed-date shipping. If you have a time-sensitive need — a trip leaving soon — email support@trailheadsupply.co before ordering and we will tell you honestly whether your gear can arrive in time given normal processing and transit.

SECTION 7 — LOST OR DELAYED SHIPMENTS

Most packages arrive on time, but carriers occasionally lose or delay a shipment. Here is what to do if your gear has not shown up.

First, check your tracking. The tracking number we emailed you is the best source of truth for where your package is. Carrier tracking sometimes pauses for a day or two between scans without anything being wrong — a package can sit at a regional facility over a weekend and then move again Monday morning.

If tracking shows no movement for five business days, or if tracking shows the package as delivered but you cannot find it, contact support@trailheadsupply.co or call (307) 367-4180. Have your order number ready. We will open an inquiry with the carrier on your behalf.

For a package marked delivered but missing, please also check around your property — porches, side doors, with neighbors, and the area near your mailbox — and confirm the shipping address on your order was correct. Carriers occasionally mark a package delivered a little early or leave it in an unexpected spot.

If the carrier confirms a package is lost in transit, we will send a replacement at no cost to you or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer. We handle the carrier claim ourselves — that is our job, not yours.

The sooner you tell us about a problem, the more options we have to fix it, so please do not wait weeks to reach out. A quick email the moment something seems off is always the right call.

A little more detail on timing and expectations, so you know when patience is warranted and when it is time to contact us.

Transit times vary by zone. A Zone 1 order within Wyoming typically arrives faster than a Zone 3 order crossing the country. Standard shipping is not a guaranteed-date service, so think of carrier estimates as estimates, not promises. Weather in the mountains, holiday volume, and carrier backlogs can all add a day or two.

Weather delays are real here. We ship from the Wind River country, and winter storms can slow pickups and regional transit. When a major storm is moving through, we will note it on trailheadsupply.co, and we appreciate your patience — the same mountains that delay a package are the reason you bought the gear.

Address problems cause most delays. If a package is returned to us as undeliverable because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will contact you to confirm the correct address and reship. A second shipment due to an address error may incur the standard shipping rate again, so please check your address carefully at checkout.

If you have moved or need to redirect a package mid-transit, contact us right away. Once a carrier has the package, redirection is sometimes possible but not guaranteed, and it works best when we catch it early. We will do what we can with the carrier on your behalf.

SECTION 8 — INTERNATIONAL ORDERS

At this time, Trailhead Supply Co. ships within the United States only. We do not currently offer international shipping.

Our shipping zones, as described in Section 6, cover all fifty states: the contiguous United States across Zones 1, 2, and 3, plus Alaska and Hawaii at the Zone 3 rate with the stated surcharge. Orders to United States addresses, including APO and FPO military addresses where the carrier supports delivery, are welcome.

If you are outside the United States and would like to order from us, we are sorry that we cannot ship to you directly yet. We are a small, independent shop, and the customs paperwork, duties, and carrier requirements for international shipping are more than we can take on reliably right now. We would rather not offer a service than offer one we cannot do well.

A few customers have asked about using a US-based package-forwarding service — shipping to a domestic address that then forwards the package abroad. We can ship to a valid US address, and what happens after that is between you and your forwarding service. Please note that once a package leaves our hands and arrives at the US address you provided, our shipping responsibility ends; we cannot track or replace a package through an onward international leg.

If and when we are able to offer international shipping in the future, we will announce it on trailheadsupply.co. Until then, thank you for understanding the limits of a small mountain-town shop.
