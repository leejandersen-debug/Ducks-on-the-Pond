How to grade

  Retrieval, first: run the question and read only the retrieved list. HIT if
  an expected source chunk is anywhere in the top 3, MISS if not. A
  cross-document item needs a chunk from both documents. A refusal item takes
  a dash for retrieval.

  Answer, second: PASS only if the claim matches the expected answer AND the
  cited source is one of the accepted sources. A cited section passes if it is
  either the true section or the label of the chunk that holds the passage.
  The chunker labels each chunk with the first section it contains, so a
  correct passage can carry a neighbouring section's label. Accepted labels
  are listed for each item.

  Refusal items pass only with the exact reply "I don't know". Any confident
  answer fails.

  Score the two measures separately, out of 12 each. Never average them.

2. TAKING BACK A SALE

Question: I marked a card as sold by accident. Can I take that back?

Expected answer: Yes. Click "Undo sale" in the Sold table. The card returns
to your inventory, but it stays not tradeable until you turn Tradeable back
on.

Source: users-guide.txt, 6. MY COLLECTION › 6.5 Recording a sale.
  Accepted chunk label: 6. MY COLLECTION › 6.1 Adding a card (chunk
  users-guide-03).
  Also accepted: faq.txt, MY COLLECTION (chunk faq-18); glossary.txt, "Undo
  sale" (chunk glossary-04).

Pass: says yes and names "Undo sale".
Fail: "no", "delete and re-add it", or "contact support" as the way to do it.
Vocabulary note: "by accident" and "take that back" stand in for the
documents' "entered by mistake" and "reverse".

3. WHO SEES WHAT I PAID

Question: Can other people on the app see how much I paid for my cards?

Expected answer: No. Other collectors cannot see your paid prices. They see
only your username, your tradeable cards (including grade, serial and cert
number) and your public wants. Admins can see all collection data, and use it
only for serial-number conflicts and support requests.

Source: privacy-notice.txt, 5. WHO CAN SEE IT › 5.1 Other collectors.
  Accepted chunk label: 3. WHAT WE COLLECT › 3.2 Information collected
  automatically (chunk privacy-notice-01).
  Also accepted: customer-service-policy.txt, 10. PRIVACY AND YOUR DATA (chunk
  customer-service-policy-05); terms-of-use.txt, 6. YOUR CONTENT › 6.3 What
  other collectors can see or 5. WHAT THE APPLICATION DOES AND DOES NOT DO
  (chunk terms-of-use-02).

Pass: answers no for other collectors.
Fail: "yes", or "only if the card is tradeable". Tradeable cards are visible,
but never their paid price.
Vocabulary note: "other people on the app" and "how much I paid" stand in for
"other collectors" and "paid prices".

4. TURNING TRADEABLE OFF

Question: If I decide I don't want to trade a card anymore, what happens to
the offers people already sent for it?

Expected answer: Turning the card's Tradeable setting off automatically
cancels any open trade offers that include it.

Source: users-guide.txt, 6. MY COLLECTION › 6.3 Making a card tradeable.
  Accepted chunk label: 6. MY COLLECTION › 6.1 Adding a card (chunk
  users-guide-03).
  Also accepted: faq.txt, TRADING ("Why was my trade cancelled when nobody
  cancelled it?") (chunk faq-36); capabilities-summary.txt, 6. TRADING (chunk
  capabilities-summary-02); glossary.txt, "tradeable" (chunk glossary-04).

Pass: says the open offers that include the card are cancelled
automatically.
Fail: the offers stay open, must be declined one by one, or are "paused".
Vocabulary note: meaning-only. The question never says "Tradeable", "turn
off" or "cancel".

5. WHAT /50 MEANS

Question: What does it mean when a card says /50?

Expected answer: It is the print run: only 50 copies of that parallel (or
variant) were made. Each one is stamped with its own serial number, and a
serial number cannot be higher than the print run.

Source: glossary.txt, "print run" (and "numbered card").
  Accepted chunk label: DUCKS ON THE POND CARDS GLOSSARY (chunk glossary-02).
  Also accepted: users-guide.txt, 4. KEY TERMS ("Parallel" and "Serial number")
  or 3. SIGNING IN (TEST USERS) (chunk users-guide-01); faq.txt, SERIAL
  NUMBERS ("Serial N is higher than the print run", chunk faq-25). [v2]

Pass: says only 50 were made (the print run).
Fail: a grade, a price, a card number in the set, or "50 in stock".

      SERIAL NUMBERS AND DISPUTES (chunk collector-handbook-03).
  (a)+(b) faq.txt, PRIVACY ("Who can read the message I send with a trade
      offer?", chunk faq-51) holds both halves, so it alone counts. [v2]

payment or address details.

10. MONTHLY REFUND (PRECISION: MONTHLY vs YEARLY)

Question: I'm on the monthly Flock plan and cancelled two days into the
month. Do I get money back for the rest of the month?

Expected answer: No. Monthly plans get no refund for a month that has
started. The plan stays active until the end of the paid month, then the
account moves to the free Pond plan. (Only charges made in error are always
refunded.)

Source: pricing-guide.txt, 9. REFUNDS (and 8.1 How to cancel).
  Accepted chunk label: 8. CANCELLING › 8.1 How to cancel (chunk
  pricing-guide-03).

Pass: answers no refund for the started month.
Fail: a full or partial refund, or the yearly plan's 14-day full refund
applied to a monthly plan.
Why it is a precision item: the yearly plan's 14-day full refund sits in the
same list, and a 14-day free trial appears elsewhere in the guide.
Corpus note: the Pricing Guide is a fictional SAMPLE (the app is free). The
item tests whether the engine reads the document correctly, not real prices.
