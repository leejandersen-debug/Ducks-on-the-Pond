CARD SHOW PACKING TIPS

Heading to a card show this weekend? A little preparation keeps your cards
safe and your trades moving.

Pack every raw card in a penny sleeve and a top loader, and keep graded slabs
upright in a padded slab box so the cases don't scratch. Bring a small binder
of the cards you have marked tradeable, so other collectors can browse them at
the table without handling your best pieces.

Before you leave, check your wishlist and write down the serial numbers of any
numbered cards you are hunting for, so you can check a card in hand against
what is already recorded.
