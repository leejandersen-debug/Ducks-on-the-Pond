# Community Newsletter Draft — Seasonal Gear Care Tips

Wash technical shells cold with a technical cleaner — never fabric
softener, which clogs the membrane. Re-proof the water-repellent
coating after every 10–12 washes.

Store sleeping bags uncompressed in the large mesh sack, never the
stuff sack they shipped in.
