1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Indemnification Policy explains who is responsible when a claim, loss or
legal cost arises from the use of the Ducks on the Pond Cards application
("the Application"). It forms part of the Terms of Use and applies to every
person who creates an account, adds catalog or collection data, lists cards,
or takes part in trades through the Application ("you" or "Collector").

"Ducks on the Pond Cards", "we", "us" and "our" mean the operator of the
Application, together with its owners, officers, employees, contractors and
agents.

This policy should be read together with the Customer Service Policy
(customer-service-policy.txt) and the User's Guide (users-guide.txt).


2. DEFINITIONS

  Claim       Any demand, complaint, lawsuit, arbitration, regulatory inquiry
              or other proceeding, whether threatened or actual.

  Losses      Damages, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and
              expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs.

  Collector   A registered user of the Application, including test users in
              a test environment.

  Collector   Any information a Collector enters, including catalog entries,
  Content     card copies, serial numbers, grades, cert numbers, prices,
              wishlist notes, trade messages and photos.

  Trade       An offer, counteroffer or completed exchange of cards between
              Collectors recorded in the Application.


3. NATURE OF THE SERVICE

The Application is a record-keeping and matching tool. In particular:

  a. We do not buy, sell, hold, ship, insure, authenticate or grade cards.
  b. We do not process payments. Paid prices, listed prices and sold prices
     are records entered by Collectors.
  c. Trades are agreements made directly between Collectors. Recording a trade
     as complete in the Application does not mean we have verified that any
     card was delivered or matched its description.
  d. Market prices shown in reports are for reference only and are not an
     appraisal, a guarantee of value, or an offer to buy.
  e. Serial-number conflict reviews are administrative decisions about
     records in the Application. They are not a legal finding of ownership or
     authenticity.


4. YOUR OBLIGATION TO INDEMNIFY US

You agree to defend, indemnify and hold harmless Ducks on the Pond Cards from
and against any Claims and Losses arising out of or relating to:

  a. Your use or misuse of the Application.
  b. Collector Content you submit, including any inaccurate, misleading or

     number, condition or price.
  c. Any Trade or sale you take part in, including failure to deliver a card,
     delivery of a card that does not match its description, loss or damage
     in transit, and any dispute with another Collector.
  d. Any counterfeit, altered, stolen or misrepresented card you list,
     describe or trade.
  e. Your breach of the Terms of Use, the Customer Service Policy or this
     policy.
  f. Your violation of any law or regulation, including laws on consumer
     protection, taxes on sales, import and export, and fraud.
  g. Your infringement of another person's intellectual property, privacy or
     publicity rights, for example by uploading card photos or images you do
     not have the right to use.
  h. Access to the Application through your account by anyone you allowed to
     use it.


5. OUR OBLIGATION TO INDEMNIFY YOU

Ducks on the Pond Cards will defend and indemnify you against third-party
Claims that the Application's software itself, as provided by us and used as
intended, infringes a third party's copyright or trademark.

This obligation does not apply to Claims arising from Collector Content,
Trades, or your combination of the Application with other software or data.

If such a Claim is made, we may, at our option: obtain the right for you to
keep using the Application; modify it so it no longer infringes; or suspend
the affected feature. This section states our entire liability for
infringement Claims.


6. EXCLUSIONS

Neither party is required to indemnify the other for Losses to the extent
they were caused by the other party's own fraud, gross negligence or wilful
misconduct.

We are not responsible for:

  a. The accuracy of catalog data added by Collectors.
  b. Any Collector's failure to honor a Trade.
  c. The value, condition, authenticity or grade of any card.
  d. Decisions made in serial-number conflict reviews, provided they were
     made in good faith on the information available.
  e. Loss of data in a test environment, which may be reset without notice.
  f. Interruptions caused by third-party hosting, database or network

8. INDEMNIFICATION PROCEDURE

A party seeking indemnification ("the Indemnified Party") must:

  1. Give the other party ("the Indemnifying Party") written notice of the
     Claim within 30 days of learning about it. A late notice only reduces
     the obligation to the extent the delay causes harm.
  2. Allow the Indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of
     the Claim, using counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party.
  3. Provide reasonable cooperation and information, at the Indemnifying
     Party's expense.

The Indemnifying Party may not settle a Claim in a way that admits fault on
behalf of the Indemnified Party, or imposes obligations on it, without its
written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

The Indemnified Party may take part in the defense with its own counsel at
its own expense.

9. DISPUTES BETWEEN COLLECTORS

Disputes between Collectors about Trades should first follow the trade dispute
process in section 6.4 of the Customer Service Policy. We may help by
reviewing records and correcting ownership data, but we are not a party to
any Trade and do not act as an arbitrator, escrow agent or guarantor.

If you bring a Claim against us that arises from a dispute with another
Collector, you release us from that Claim, except to the extent it results
from our own fraud, gross negligence or wilful misconduct.


10. ACCOUNT ACTION

If a Claim against us arises from your conduct, we may restrict, suspend or
close your account while the Claim is investigated, as described in section 9
of the Customer Service Policy. Doing so does not end your obligations under
this policy.


11. SURVIVAL

Your obligations under this policy continue after your account is closed or
deleted, for any Claim relating to activity that happened while the account
was open.
