3. WHAT WE COLLECT

3.1 Information you enter

  - Account details: your username. (Once personal accounts arrive: your
    email address and sign-in details. [TO CONFIRM])
  - Collection: the cards you own, serial numbers, grading company, grade,
    cert number, price paid, date acquired, listed price, sale price and date,
    and condition notes.
  - Wishlist: the cards you want, minimum grade, maximum price, notes, and
    whether each want is public or private.
  - Trades: offers you make and receive, the cards in them, their status, and
    trade messages.
  - Serial-number reviews: your note for the admin, and the admin's decision
    and note.
  - Catalog entries you add: sets, parallels, cards and variants.
  - Search questions you type on the Search page.
  - Anything you send to support.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  - Server logs. For every request we record the page or API route (the path
    only, never the part after "?"), the method, the response status and how
    long it took. Search requests also record the question you typed and how
    many results came back. Errors are recorded with a short error code.
  - Log protection. The logger removes values whose names suggest secrets,
    such as passwords, tokens, cookies, session IDs, API keys, email
    addresses, request bodies, card numbers and database connection details,
    before anything is written.
  - Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and visitors.
    [TO CONFIRM: confirm with the provider's current documentation that this
    is cookie-free and does not store IP addresses.]
  - Test user choice. A cookie remembers which test user you picked.

We do not collect payment card details, bank details, precise location,
contacts, or photos. (Card photos are planned but not built.)


4. HOW WE USE IT

  - To run the Application: show your collection, find wishlist matches,
    record trades and produce your reports.
  - To protect serial numbers and resolve serial-number conflicts.
  - To answer support requests and investigate trade disputes.
  - To keep the Application secure and fix errors.
  - To understand which pages are used, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for
advertising.


5. WHO CAN SEE IT

5.1 Other collectors

Other collectors can see:

  - your username;
  - your tradeable cards, including grade, serial number and cert number;
  - your public wants.

They cannot see your paid prices, listed prices, acquired dates, private
wants, non-tradeable cards or sold records. Only the two collectors in a trade
can see that trade and its message.

When a card moves to another collector through a trade, your paid and listed
prices are removed from it, and its acquired date is reset.

5.2 Admins and support staff

Admins can see all collection data. They use it only to resolve
serial-number conflicts and support requests. Support staff look at your data
only to answer your request.

5.3 Service providers

We use these providers to run the Application. Each processes data only on our
instructions:

  - Vercel: hosting, server logs and web analytics.
  - Neon: the Postgres database where Application data is stored.

Search works by turning your question into numbers (an embedding) inside the
Application itself, so your search question is not sent to an outside AI
service. A separate developer tool can send questions and reference-document
passages to Anthropic's Claude model, but it is not part of the web
Application and never sends collection data. [TO CONFIRM provider list before
publication.]

5.4 When the law requires it

We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of
collectors or the public.


6. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

  - Collection, wishlist and trade data: while your account is open.
  - Trade records involving another collector: kept after you leave, with your
    details removed, so the other collector's history stays accurate.
  - Catalog entries: kept as part of the shared catalog, without your name.
  - Server logs: [TO CONFIRM, for example 30 days].
  - Support conversations: [TO CONFIRM, for example 2 years].
  - Test environments: may be reset at any time.


7. HOW WE PROTECT IT

  - Database row-level security, so each collector can change only their own
    cards and wants, and can see only trades they are part of.
  - Encrypted connections to the database, with the server certificate
    checked.
  - Error messages never show database host or account details.
  - Development tools refuse to run against the production database.

See the Security and Incident Response document
(security-incident-response.txt) for more, including what we do if data is
exposed.

  - Ask us to delete your account and collection.
