1. PURPOSE

This policy explains how Ducks on the Pond Cards supports the collectors who
use the application: what we help with, how to reach us, how quickly we
respond, and how we handle trade problems, serial-number disputes, account
questions and complaints.

For how to use the application itself, see the Ducks on the Pond Cards User's
Guide (users-guide.txt).


2. OUR SERVICE COMMITMENTS

  - Be courteous, patient and plain-spoken with every collector.
  - Respond within the times in section 5.
  - Treat every collector the same way, whatever the size or value of their
    collection.
  - Never ask for a password, a payment card number or a bank detail.
  - Explain decisions, especially when the answer is no.
  - Protect collectors' personal and collection information (section 10).


3. WHAT WE HELP WITH

Customer service covers:

  - Using the application: catalog, collection, wishlist, trades, reports and
    search.
  - Errors and messages shown by the application.
  - Catalog corrections: wrong set details, missing parallels, cards or
    variants, wrong print runs.
  - Serial-number conflicts and their admin review.
  - Trade problems inside the application, such as a trade that cannot be
    completed or a card that moved unexpectedly.
  - Account and test-user access.
  - Requests to correct or delete your data.
  - Complaints about the application or about our service.

What we do not cover:

  - Payments. The application does not process payments. Sale prices and paid
    prices are records you enter yourself. We cannot refund, charge or
    reverse money between collectors.
  - Shipping or handing over physical cards. Trading partners arrange this
    between themselves.
  - Card authentication or grading. Contact the grading company (PSA, BGS, SGC
    or CGC) about a grade or cert number.
  - Valuations. Market prices shown in reports are for reference only and are
    not an appraisal or an offer to buy.

6. TRADES

6.1 How trades work

Trades happen between collectors. The application records offers and, once
both sides agree, moves the cards to their new owners when either collector
clicks "Complete (cards exchanged)". Only complete a trade after the physical
cards have actually been exchanged.

6.2 Before you complete a trade

  - Agree the delivery method with your trading partner using the trade
    message.
  - Check the cards you receive against the trade: set, card number, parallel,
    serial number, grade and cert number.
  - Do not click Complete until you have the cards you were promised.

6.3 Trade problems we can help with

  - A trade that will not complete. The most common reason is that a card in
    it was sold, turned off as tradeable, or moved in another trade first. The
    application cancels or blocks those trades on purpose.
  - A trade completed by mistake before the cards were exchanged.
  - A card received that does not match the trade.

6.4 Trade disputes

If you and your trading partner disagree about a completed trade:

  1. Contact the other collector first, through a new trade message or your
     agreed channel, and try to resolve it between you.
  2. If that does not work within 7 days, contact us with the details in
     section 4 and any evidence (photos of the cards received, messages).
  3. We will contact both collectors, review the trade record, and reply with
     our findings within 10 business days.

What we can do: correct the ownership records in the application when both
collectors agree, or when the evidence clearly shows a recording mistake.

What we cannot do: force a collector to send or return a physical card,
recover a card lost in the post, or pay compensation.

If we find that a collector deliberately misrepresented a card or did not send
cards for completed trades, we may restrict or close their account (section
9).


7. SERIAL-NUMBER CONFLICTS

Only one unsold copy of a numbered variant can hold a given serial number. If
you add a serial number that another collection already holds, your card goes
to an admin for review instead of being added straight away.

How we review:

  - We aim to review every conflict within 5 business days.
  - We compare the two entries and may ask either collector for a clear photo
    of the front and back of the card showing the serial number, and the cert
    number for a graded card.
  - If one entry is a typo or a duplicate, we correct or remove it and approve
    the valid one.
  - If both collectors appear to hold the same serial, we ask both for
    evidence and decide based on what they send. A grading company cert that
    records the serial number is the strongest evidence.
  - If a collector does not reply within 14 days, we decide on the evidence we
    have.

You will see the outcome, and the admin's note explaining it, under "My
serial-number reviews" in My collection. If you disagree, ask for a second
review (section 11).

Email notifications for reviews are not yet available, so check My collection
for updates.


8. CATALOG CORRECTIONS

Any collector can add sets, parallels, cards and variants. If you find a
mistake you cannot fix yourself, such as a wrong print run or a duplicated set,
send us the set, the card number, what is wrong and a source if you have one
(for example a manufacturer checklist). We confirm the correction before
changing it, because a catalog change affects every collector's records.


9. ACCOUNTS AND ACCEPTABLE USE

Collectors must:

  - Enter honest information about the cards they own.
  - Only mark cards as tradeable if they actually have them and are willing to
    trade them.
  - Complete trades only after the cards have been exchanged.
  - Be respectful in trade messages and wishlist notes.

We may warn, restrict or close an account for: misrepresenting cards,
repeatedly failing to deliver on completed trades, abusive messages, or
deliberately entering false serial numbers. Unless there is a risk to other
collectors, we warn first and give the collector a chance to respond.

Test environment note: the current release uses test users (Alice, Bob and
Carol) instead of personal accounts. Data in a test environment may be reset
without notice.


10. PRIVACY AND YOUR DATA

  - Other collectors can see your username, your tradeable cards (including
    grade, serial and cert number) and your public wants. The application
    does not show them your paid prices, listed prices, acquired dates,
    private wants, non-tradeable cards or sold records.
  - Only the two collectors in a trade can see that trade and its message.
  - Admins can see all collection data, and use it only to resolve
    serial-number conflicts and support requests.
  - When a card moves to another collector through a trade, your paid and
    listed prices are removed from it.
  - Support staff only look at your collection data to answer your request.
  - You can ask us to correct your data, or to delete your account and
    collection. We confirm your identity first and complete the request
    within 30 days. Trade records involving other collectors may be kept, with
    your details removed, so their histories stay accurate.

11. COMPLAINTS AND ESCALATION

If you are unhappy with our service or a decision:

  1. Reply to your support conversation and say you want to make a complaint.
     We acknowledge complaints within 2 business days.
  2. A team member who was not involved in the original decision reviews it
     and replies within 10 business days.
  3. If you are still unhappy, ask for it to go to the support lead [TO
     CONFIRM name or role], whose decision is final.

We record every complaint and review them each month to improve the
application and this policy.


12. FOR SUPPORT STAFF

  - Reply first with a summary of the problem in your own words, so the
    collector knows you understood it.
  - Check the User's Guide's "Common messages" section before escalating an
    error.
  - Never change another collector's cards, serial numbers or trades without a
    clear reason recorded in the admin note.
  - When approving or rejecting a serial-number conflict, always write a note
    the collector will understand.
  - Escalate to engineering any message that says "Something went wrong. It
    has been logged as action_failed." Include the time it happened, so the
    log entry can be found.
  - Escalate any suspected data exposure as Urgent immediately.


13. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We review this policy at least every 6 months and whenever the application
changes how trades, sales or accounts work. Significant changes are announced
in the application at least 14 days before they take effect.
