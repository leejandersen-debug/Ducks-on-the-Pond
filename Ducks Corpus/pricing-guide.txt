DUCKS ON THE POND CARDS
SUBSCRIPTION PRICING GUIDE

Version: 1.0 SAMPLE, November 2026

SAMPLE DOCUMENT. This pricing guide is fictional. It was written as test
content for the Ducks Corpus. The Ducks on the Pond Cards application is
currently free, does not sell subscriptions and does not process payments.
None of the plans, prices or terms below are on offer. Do not quote them to
a collector or publish them as real prices.


1. PURPOSE

This guide describes the subscription plans for the Ducks on the Pond Cards
application ("the Application"): what each plan costs, what it includes, how
billing works, and how to upgrade, downgrade, cancel or ask for a refund.

For how to use the Application, see the User's Guide (users-guide.txt). For
how to reach support, see the Customer Service Policy
(customer-service-policy.txt).


2. PLANS AT A GLANCE

  Plan          Monthly     Yearly       Best for
  ------------  ----------  -----------  ---------------------------------
  Pond          Free        Free         Trying the app, small collections
  Flock         US$4.99     US$49.00     Active collectors and traders
  Wetland       US$12.99    US$129.00    Dealers and very large collections

Yearly prices work out to about two months free compared with paying monthly.
Prices are in US dollars and do not include sales tax or VAT, which is added
at checkout where it applies (section 6.4).


3. WHAT EACH PLAN INCLUDES

3.1 Features in every plan

Every plan, including the free Pond plan, includes:

  - Browsing the full card catalog.
  - Serial-number protection: no two collectors can record the same serial
    of the same card.
  - Trading: propose, accept, decline, counter, cancel and complete trades,
    with the same safeguards on every plan.
  - The Summary report.
  - Document search over the help documents.
  - Support by email under the Customer Service Policy.

No plan changes how trades work, who wins a serial-number dispute, or what
other collectors can see about your cards. Paying for a plan never gives a
collector priority over another collector in a trade or a review.

3.2 Limits by plan

  Feature                          Pond       Flock        Wetland
  -------------------------------  ---------  -----------  -----------
  Card copies in your collection   100        2,500        Unlimited
  Wishlist entries                 10         250          Unlimited
  Wishlist matching                Weekly     Daily        Daily
  Open trades at one time          3          25           100
  Listed (for sale) cards          10         500          Unlimited
  Reports                          Summary    All reports  All reports
  Document searches per day        20         200          1,000
  Support response target          Standard   Standard     Priority

"All reports" means the Summary, Inventory, For sale and Sold reports
described in section 9 of the User's Guide.

"Priority" support means Wetland requests are answered first within the
response targets in section 5 of the Customer Service Policy. It does not
change the outcome of a dispute.

3.3 Planned features (not included in any price today)

The following are planned and are not part of any plan until announced:
card photos, market-price entry, email or push notifications, and data
export. When one of these launches, this guide will say which plans include
it. A plan's price will not rise during a paid period because a new feature
is added.


4. CHOOSING A PLAN

  - Fewer than 100 cards and a few trades at a time: Pond.
  - A growing collection, regular trading, or you want the Inventory, For
    sale and Sold reports: Flock.
  - Thousands of cards, many listed cards, or many trades open at once:
    Wetland.

You can change plans at any time (section 7), so starting on Pond and
upgrading later costs nothing extra.


5. FREE TRIAL

New accounts can try Flock or Wetland free for 14 days. One trial per
collector. No payment card is needed to start the trial. When the trial ends
without a plan being chosen, the account returns to Pond (section 8.2 explains
what happens to anything over the Pond limits).


6. BILLING

6.1 Billing periods

Monthly plans renew every month on the day you subscribed. Yearly plans renew
every 12 months on the same date. You can see your renewal date on the Account
page.

6.2 Payment methods

Payments are taken by a third-party payment processor. Ducks on the Pond Cards
never sees or stores your full card number. Support staff will never ask for a
payment card number, password or bank detail, by email or any other way.

6.3 Renewal reminders

Yearly subscribers get a reminder 14 days before renewal. Monthly subscribers
see the next charge and date on the Account page.

6.4 Taxes

Sales tax, VAT or GST is added where the law requires it, based on your billing
address. The tax amount is shown before you confirm a purchase.

6.5 Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we try again after 3 days and after 7 days. The
plan stays active during those 7 days. If the payment still fails, the account
moves to Pond as described in section 8.2.

6.6 Price changes

We will give at least 30 days' notice before a price change applies to you.
A new price applies from your next renewal, never to a period you have already
paid for. Yearly subscribers keep their current price until their next yearly
renewal.


7. CHANGING PLANS

7.1 Upgrading

An upgrade takes effect straight away. You are charged the difference for the
rest of the current billing period (a prorated charge), and the new price
applies from your next renewal.

  Example: halfway through a US$4.99 Flock month you move to Wetland
  (US$12.99). You pay about half the US$8.00 difference, US$4.00, now, then
  US$12.99 at the next renewal.

7.2 Downgrading

A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You keep
the higher plan's limits until then. There is no partial refund for the
remaining time.

7.3 Switching between monthly and yearly

Monthly to yearly takes effect straight away, with the unused part of the
month credited toward the yearly price. Yearly to monthly takes effect at the
next yearly renewal.


8. CANCELLING

8.1 How to cancel

Cancel at any time from the Account page. You do not need to contact support
to cancel. The plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid
for, and then the account moves to Pond.

8.2 What happens to your data over the Pond limits

Nothing is deleted when an account moves to Pond. If you have more than the
Pond limits:

  - Card copies over 100 stay in your collection and in your reports, but
    you cannot add new copies until you are under the limit.
  - Wishlist entries over 10 stay saved but are paused from matching. You
    choose which 10 stay active.
  - Open trades stay open and can be completed, but you cannot propose new
    trades while more than 3 are open.
  - Listed prices over the limit of 10 are kept, but only the 10 most
    recently listed cards appear in the For sale report.

Serial numbers you have recorded stay protected on every plan.

8.3 Closing your account

Closing an account is different from cancelling a plan. See the Customer
Service Policy section 10 for account deletion and what happens to your data.


9. REFUNDS

  - Yearly plans: a full refund if you ask within 14 days of the first
    yearly charge or of a yearly renewal.
  - Monthly plans: no refund for a month that has started, except as below.
  - Charges made in error (for example, a double charge, or a renewal after
    you cancelled) are always refunded in full.
  - If the Application is unavailable for more than 72 hours in a row
    because of a problem on our side, paid subscribers get a credit for the
    lost days on request.

To ask for a refund, contact support using the details in section 4 of the
Customer Service Policy. Include the date and amount of the charge. Do not
send your payment card number. Refunds go back to the original payment method
and usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days.

Refunds cover subscription fees only. The Application does not process card
sales or trades between collectors, so we cannot refund, charge or recover
money for a sale or trade (see Customer Service Policy section 3).


10. DISCOUNTS

  - Yearly billing: about two months free compared with monthly (section 2).
  - Card shows and clubs: a club with 10 or more members can ask for 20% off
    Flock for its members.
  - Discounts cannot be combined, and they do not apply to taxes.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Is there still a free plan?
A: Yes. Pond is free with no time limit.

Q: Do paid plans get better trade terms or win serial-number disputes?
A: No. Trading rules and dispute reviews are the same on every plan
(section 3.1).

Q: Will I lose cards if I stop paying?
A: No. Nothing is deleted when an account moves to Pond. Some actions are
limited until you are under the Pond limits (section 8.2).

Q: Can I pay for a card sale or trade through the Application?
A: No. Subscriptions are the only thing the Application charges for. Prices
you enter for cards are your own records.

Q: Does the price include tax?
A: No. Tax is added at checkout where it applies (section 6.4).
