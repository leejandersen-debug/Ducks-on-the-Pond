DUCKS ON THE POND CARDS
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Version: November 2026 (test harness release)

Related documents: User's Guide (users-guide.txt), Capabilities Summary
(capabilities-summary.txt), Customer Service Policy
(customer-service-policy.txt).


GETTING STARTED

Q: What is Ducks on the Pond Cards?
A: A web application for sports card collectors. You can record the cards you
   own, keep a wishlist, find collectors who have the cards you want, trade
   with them, and see reports on your inventory, sales and profit.

Q: Does it cost anything?
A: No. The application is currently free.

Q: How do I sign in?
A: There are no personal accounts yet. Use the "Test user" drop-down at the
   top of any page, choose Alice, Bob or Carol, and click "Switch".

Q: Which test user should I pick?
A: Alice and Bob are collectors. Carol is an admin and is the only one who can
   use the Admin page. To try a trade, act as one collector, then switch to
   the other to respond.

Q: Why does the page say "Test login is off in this environment"?
A: Test sign-in has been turned off where you are, usually in production. You
   can browse the catalog but cannot add or change anything.

Q: Why does a page say "Pick a test user first"?
A: My collection, Wishlist, Trades and Reports belong to a particular
   collector. Choose a test user at the top of the page and try again.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: Not yet. The web application works in a phone's browser.


THE CATALOG

Q: What is the difference between a set, a parallel, a card and a variant?
A: A set is a product release, such as 2024 Topps Chrome. A parallel is a
   version of the set's cards, such as Base or Gold Refractor /50. A card is
   one numbered card in the set, such as #150 Shohei Ohtani. A variant is a
   specific card in a specific parallel, optionally with an autograph or
   relic. You add variants to your collection.

Q: The card I own is not in the catalog. What do I do?
A: Add it. Go to Catalog, open or add the set, add the parallel if it is
   missing, add the card, then add the variant. Anyone signed in can add to
   the catalog.

Q: Why does every set already have a Base parallel?
A: The application creates a Base parallel for every new set, and a Base
   variant for every new card, so you can add base cards straight away.

Q: I made a mistake in the catalog. Can I edit or delete it?
A: Not from the interface yet. Contact support with the set, card number and
   what is wrong. Catalog changes affect every collector, so support confirms
   corrections before making them.

Q: Why did I get "That already exists"?
A: A set, parallel, card or variant with the same details is already in the
   catalog. Look for it in the list instead of adding it again.


MY COLLECTION

Q: How do I add a card I own?
A: Go to My collection, choose the variant under "Add a card to my
   collection", fill in any details you have (serial number, grade, cert
   number, price paid, date acquired, condition) and click "Add card".

Q: Which grading companies are supported?
A: PSA, BGS, SGC and CGC. Grades go from 1 to 10 in half steps. Choose "Raw
   (ungraded)" for an ungraded card.

Q: Why do I get "Enter both a grading company and a grade, or neither"?
A: A graded card needs both its company and its grade. For a raw card, leave
   both blank.

Q: Can I edit a card after adding it?
A: You can change its Tradeable setting and its listed price, mark it sold,
   or undo a sale. The grade, serial number, cert number and price paid cannot
   be edited yet. Delete the card and add it again with the right details.

Q: What is the difference between Delete and Mark sold?
A: Delete removes the card permanently. Mark sold keeps it as a sale record,
   so it shows in your Sold report with its profit. Use Mark sold if you sold
   the card.

Q: I marked a card sold by mistake. Can I undo it?
A: Yes. Click "Undo sale" in the Sold table. The card returns to your
   collection. It comes back not tradeable, so turn Tradeable on again if you
   want to offer it.

Q: Can I upload photos of my cards?
A: Not yet. Photo upload is planned but not built.


SERIAL NUMBERS

Q: What is a serial number?
A: The number stamped on a numbered card, for example 23 on a card numbered
   23/50. Enter just the 23.

Q: Why was my card "sent to an admin for review"?
A: Another collector already holds an unsold copy of the same variant with
   that serial number. Only one copy of each serial can exist, so an admin
   checks which entry is correct.

Q: How long does a serial-number review take?
A: The aim is 5 business days. Check the status under "My serial-number
   reviews" on the My collection page, as review emails are not set up yet.

Q: What happens during a review?
A: The admin compares both entries and may ask either collector for a photo
   of the card's front and back. If one entry is a typo, it is corrected and
   the valid one approved. The admin's note explains the decision.

Q: What if I disagree with the decision?
A: Ask for a second review. See section 11 of the Customer Service Policy.

Q: Why do I get "Serial N is higher than the print run"?
A: The serial you entered is larger than the number made, for example 51 on a
   /50 card. Check the number, and check you chose the right variant.


WISHLIST

Q: What is the difference between "Any version of a card" and "An exact
   variant"?
A: "Any version" matches every parallel and variant of that card. "An exact
   variant" matches only that one, for example only the Gold Refractor /50.

Q: Can other collectors see my wishlist?
A: Only wants marked Public. Public is ticked by default. Untick it to keep a
   want private.

Q: Why are no matches showing?
A: Matches only include other collectors' cards that are marked tradeable and
   meet your minimum grade and grading company, if you set them. Try loosening
   the minimum grade, or check back later.

Q: Does the max price filter matches?
A: No. It is saved with your want for reference, but matching uses the card
   and grade only.


TRADING

Q: How do I start a trade?
A: Go to Trades, choose a collector under "Trade with", click "Show their
   tradeable cards", tick the cards you will give and the cards you want, and
   click "Propose trade". Or click "propose a trade" next to a match on your
   Wishlist page.

Q: Why can't I see my card in the "I give" list?
A: Only tradeable cards are listed. Turn on Tradeable for the card in My
   collection.

Q: Can a trade be one-sided?
A: Yes. You can only give or only ask, as long as at least one card is
   chosen.

Q: How do I make a counteroffer?
A: On a trade offered to you, click "Counter...". The same cards are
   preselected; change them, add a message, and click "Send counteroffer". The
   original offer is marked "countered".

Q: Who can complete a trade?
A: Either collector, once the trade is accepted. Only click "Complete (cards
   exchanged)" after you have actually received the cards.

Q: What happens when a trade is completed?
A: The cards move to their new owners in one step. Each card arrives not
   tradeable, with today's date as its acquired date, and without the previous
   owner's paid or listed price.

Q: Why was my trade cancelled when nobody cancelled it?
A: A card in it was turned off as tradeable, sold, or traded in another deal.
   The application cancels offers that include those cards.

Q: Why do I get "A card in this trade has changed owner or been sold"?
A: The same card was in another offer that was completed first, or its owner
   sold it. Propose a new trade with the cards that are still available.

Q: How do the physical cards get delivered?
A: The two collectors arrange it between themselves. The application does not
   handle shipping or payment.

Q: What if the other collector never sends the card?
A: Do not click Complete until you have received it. If a completed trade
   goes wrong, contact the other collector first, then support. See section
   6.4 of the Customer Service Policy.


SELLING AND PRICES

Q: Can I sell cards through the application?
A: You can list an asking price and record sales, but no payments go through
   the application. You arrange the sale yourself and then record it with
   "Mark sold".

Q: Why is the Market column in my reports blank?
A: Market prices can be shown, but there is no screen to enter them yet, so
   most cards have none.

Q: How is profit calculated?
A: Sold price minus the price you paid. If you did not enter a paid price,
   profit is left blank for that card.

Q: What is Margin in the For sale report?
A: Your listed price minus the price you paid.


REPORTS

Q: What reports are there?
A: A summary, an inventory report, a for-sale report and a sold report. See
   section 9 of the User's Guide.

Q: Do my reports include other collectors' cards?
A: No. Reports only show your own cards.


SEARCH

Q: What does the Search page search?
A: The Ducks on the Pond Cards help documents: the User's Guide, this FAQ,
   the Collector Handbook, Glossary, policies and release notes. It does not
   search cards or collections.

Q: Why does it say "Not in the documents"?
A: No passage was similar enough to your question. Try rewording it. The page
   shows the best score it found so you can see how close it came.

Q: Is there a length limit on questions?
A: Yes, 500 characters.


PRIVACY

Q: What can other collectors see about me?
A: Your username, your tradeable cards (including grade, serial and cert
   number) and your public wants. The application does not show them your
   prices, acquired dates, private wants, non-tradeable cards or sales.

Q: Who can see my trades?
A: Only you and the other collector in the trade. Admins cannot see trades
   they are not part of.

Q: Can admins see my collection?
A: Yes. Admins can see all collection data and use it only to resolve
   serial-number conflicts and support requests.

Q: Can I delete my data?
A: Yes. Contact support. See section 10 of the Customer Service Policy.


HELP AND SUPPORT

Q: How do I contact support?
A: See section 4 of the Customer Service Policy for contact details and what
   to include in your request.

Q: What does "Something went wrong. It has been logged as action_failed"
   mean?
A: An unexpected error happened and was recorded. Try again. If it keeps
   happening, contact support and say when it happened.

Q: Will I get an email when someone offers me a trade?
A: Not yet. Email and push notifications are not built. Check your Trades
   page.

Q: Is my test data permanent?
A: No. Data in a test environment may be reset without notice.
