DUCKS ON THE POND CARDS
SERIAL-NUMBER DISPUTE PROCEDURE

Version: 1.0 DRAFT, November 2026
Owner: Ducks on the Pond Cards support team
Applies to: collectors who record numbered cards, and the admins who review
serial-number conflicts.

This procedure expands on section 6.6 and section 10 of the User's Guide
(users-guide.txt) and section 7 of the Customer Service Policy
(customer-service-policy.txt). If they ever disagree, the Customer Service
Policy applies. Items marked [TO CONFIRM] are placeholders.


1. WHY SERIAL NUMBERS ARE PROTECTED

A numbered card is one of a limited print run, for example 23/50. Only one
physical card carries serial 23 of that variant. To keep the records honest,
the Application allows only one unsold copy of a variant to hold a given
serial number across all collections.

Sold copies do not count. If a collector marks serial 23 as sold, another
collector can then record serial 23 of that variant without a conflict.


2. WHAT IS AND IS NOT A CONFLICT

Not a conflict (rejected straight away, with a message):

  - The serial is higher than the print run.
      "Serial N is higher than the print run (/M)."
  - You already hold that serial of that variant yourself.
      "You already have serial N of this card."
  - The cert number is already recorded for that grading company.
      "That already exists."

A conflict (sent to an admin):

  - Another collector's unsold copy of the same variant already holds the
    serial. You see:
      "Serial #N is already in another collection, so this was sent to an
       admin for review."

The same serial on a different variant is not a conflict. Serial 23 of the
Gold Refractor and serial 23 of the Orange Refractor are different cards.


3. COMMON CAUSES

From most to least common [TO CONFIRM once there is real data]:

  1. A typo: one collector entered the wrong serial.
  2. The wrong variant: the card was recorded under the wrong parallel, so it
     collides with someone else's card.
  3. A stale record: the card was sold or traded outside the Application, and
     the previous owner never marked it sold.
  4. A duplicate catalog entry: the same parallel was added twice.
  5. A counterfeit or altered card.


4. WHAT TO DO AS A COLLECTOR

4.1 Before you add the card

  - Check the serial against the card itself, not a listing or a photo.
  - Check you chose the right set, card and parallel.
  - For a graded card, have the cert number ready.

4.2 When the conflict message appears

Your card is not lost. It is waiting for review. Use the "Note for admin" box
to explain, for example:

  "Card is in hand, PSA cert 12345678. Bought from a card show on
   2026-10-12. The other entry may be a typo."

Add the note when you add the card. There is no way to add or change it after
the card has been submitted.

4.3 While you wait

  - Track the review under "My serial-number reviews" at the bottom of My
    collection. Statuses are "pending admin review", "approved" and
    "rejected".
  - Watch for a support email asking for evidence (section 5). Email
    notifications from the Application are not available yet, so check My
    collection for updates.
  - If you sold or traded away the other copy yourself, under another
    account, tell support.

4.4 After the decision

  - Approved: the card is added to your collection with the details you
    submitted. It starts not tradeable.
  - Rejected: the card is not added. Read the admin's note to see why.
  - If you disagree, ask for a second review (section 8).


5. EVIDENCE

5.1 What we may ask for

  - A clear photo of the front and back of the card showing the serial
    number.
  - For a graded card, the grading company and cert number, and a photo of
    the slab label.
  - Proof of purchase or trade, if you have it (a receipt, invoice or
    message).

Crop or cover any personal details such as an address label before you send a
photo. We will never ask for a password, payment card number or bank detail.

5.2 How strong each kind of evidence is

  Strongest   A grading company cert whose record shows the serial number.
              We check the cert on the grading company's own website.
  Strong      Clear photos of the front and back that show the serial and
              match the catalog entry.
  Supporting  Receipts, invoices or messages showing when and where the card
              was bought.
  Weak        Photos from a listing, or a description with no photo.


6. HOW AN ADMIN REVIEWS A CONFLICT

6.1 The queue

Open the Admin page (admins only). It shows the 50 most recent conflicts,
open ones first. Each shows the variant, the serial, who submitted it and
when, their note and copy details, and the existing copy and its owner.

6.2 Steps

  1. Read both entries side by side. Check the variant, serial, grade and cert
     number of each.
  2. Look for an obvious recording mistake: a typo, the wrong parallel, or a
     duplicate catalog entry.
  3. If it is not obvious, contact both collectors for evidence (section 5).
  4. Wait up to 14 days for replies. If a collector does not reply in that
     time, decide on the evidence you have.
  5. Decide (section 7) and record it.
  6. Write a note the collector will understand. Every decision records who
     resolved it and when.

6.3 Recording the decision

To approve the new submission, first free the serial:

  - "Change existing copy's serial to" a corrected number, then "Save"; or
  - "Delete existing copy", if it should not exist at all.

Then enter a note and click "Approve". If the serial is still held you will
see: "Serial N is still held by a copy. Fix or delete that copy first."

To keep the existing copy, enter a note and click "Reject".

Never change or delete another collector's copy without a clear reason
written in the note.

6.4 Target times

  - First review: within 5 business days of submission.
  - Waiting for evidence: up to 14 days.
  - Second review: a reply within 10 business days of the request.


7. DECISION RULES

  Situation                                   Decision
  ------------------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  The new entry is a typo                     Reject. Note the likely correct
                                              serial so the collector can re-add
  The existing entry is a typo                Correct the existing serial,
                                              then approve
  Wrong variant on one side                   Reject that side, with a note to
                                              re-add under the right variant
  The existing owner no longer has the card   Delete the existing copy (or ask
                                              its owner to mark it sold), then
                                              approve
  Both claim the card; one has a cert that    Decide for the cert holder
  records the serial
  Both have equal evidence                    Keep the existing copy, reject,
                                              and explain; either may ask for a
                                              second review
  No evidence from either side after 14 days  Keep the existing copy, reject
  Signs of a counterfeit or altered card      Reject, and refer to the
                                              acceptable-use rules (Customer
                                              Service Policy section 9)

Deliberately entering a serial you do not hold, to block another collector,
breaks the Terms of Use (section 9) and can lead to the account being
restricted or closed.


8. SECOND REVIEW AND COMPLAINTS

If you disagree with a decision:

  1. Reply to your support conversation, or contact support, and ask for a
     second review. Include any new evidence.
  2. An admin who did not make the first decision reviews it and replies
     within 10 business days.
  3. If you are still unhappy, follow the complaints steps in section 11 of
     the Customer Service Policy. The support lead's decision is final.

An admin decision settles the Application's records. It does not decide who
legally owns a physical card.


9. KNOWN LIMITS

  - No email notifications for submitters or admins yet.
  - The "Note for admin" cannot be edited after submission.
  - A copy's serial can only be changed by an admin.
  - The queue shows the 50 most recent conflicts only.
