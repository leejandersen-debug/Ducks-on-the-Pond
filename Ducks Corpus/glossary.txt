DUCKS ON THE POND CARDS
GLOSSARY

Version: November 2026

Terms used in the Application and in its help documents, in alphabetical
order. "See also" points to related terms in this glossary. Section numbers
refer to the User's Guide (users-guide.txt) unless another document is named.


accepted
    A trade status. The recipient agreed to the offer, and it is waiting for
    the cards to be exchanged and the trade completed. See also: completed,
    trade status.

acquired date
    The date you got a card. You enter it when you add a copy. When a card
    arrives through a trade, it is set to the day the trade was completed.
    Other collectors cannot see it.

action_failed
    The code logged when an unexpected error happens. The page says
    "Something went wrong. It has been logged as action_failed." Report it
    with the time it happened.

admin
    A person allowed to use the Admin page and review serial-number
    conflicts. In the test release this is Carol.

Auto (autograph)
    A variant that carries the player's signature. See also: variant.

Base
    The standard version of the cards in a set. Every set gets a Base parallel
    automatically, and every card gets a Base variant. See also: parallel.

BGS
    Beckett Grading Services, one of the four supported grading companies.

cancelled
    A trade status. The proposer withdrew the offer, or a card in it stopped
    being tradeable or was sold.

card
    A numbered card in a set, for example #150 Shohei Ohtani in 2024 Topps
    Chrome. A card can feature one or more players. Not the same as a copy.
    See also: copy, variant.

card number
    The number printed on the back of a card within its set, such as 150 or
    RC-12. Not the same as a serial number.

catalog
    The shared list of sets, parallels, cards and variants. Anyone signed in
    can add to it. Entries cannot be edited or deleted from the pages.

centering
    How evenly a card's image sits within its borders, often written as a
    ratio such as 55/45. One of the things graders check.

cert number (cert #)
    The certification number printed on a graded card's slab label. It
    identifies that card in the grading company's records. A cert number can
    be recorded only once per grading company.

CGC
    Certified Guaranty Company, one of the four supported grading companies.

collector
    A person who uses the Application to record and trade cards.

completed
    A trade status. The cards have changed owners in the Application. Only an
    accepted trade can be completed.

condition notes
    Free-text notes about a copy's condition, such as soft corners or surface
    scratches.

copy
    One physical card that you own, recorded in My collection. A copy belongs
    to a variant and has your own details: serial, grade, cert number, price
    paid and so on.

counteroffer
    A reply to a trade offer that changes it. It starts with the original
    cards selected. Sending one marks the original offer "countered".

countered
    A trade status. The recipient replied with a counteroffer, which replaces
    this offer.

declined
    A trade status. The recipient said no.

Ducks Corpus
    The set of help documents about the Application, including this
    glossary.

For sale report
    The report of your unsold cards that have a listed price, with the margin
    over what you paid.

grade
    A grading company's score for a card's condition, from 1 to 10 in half
    steps. A grade must always come with a grading company.

graded
    A card that has been graded and sealed in a slab. See also: raw, slab.

grading company
    A business that grades cards. The Application supports PSA, BGS, SGC and
    CGC.

health check
    GET /api/health, which reports whether the Application and its database
    are up. The Home page shows the result, for example "API: ok · Database:
    ok".

Inventory report
    The report of all your unsold cards, with paid, market and listed prices.

listed price
    The asking price you set on a card you want to sell. It puts the card in
    your For sale report. Other collectors cannot see it.

margin
    Listed price minus paid price, shown in the For sale report.

market price
    A reference value for a card, shown in reports where one is known. Only
    admins can manage market prices, and there is no screen to enter them
    yet. It is not an appraisal.

match
    A tradeable copy owned by another collector that meets one of your wants,
    or one of your tradeable copies that meets another collector's public
    want.

minimum grade
    An optional condition on a want. Only copies at or above that grade, and
    from the chosen grading company if one is set, will match.

Note for admin
    A note you add when you record a numbered card, read by the admin if the
    serial is already taken. It cannot be changed after the card is
    submitted.

numbered card
    A card from a parallel with a print run, stamped with its own serial
    number, for example 23/50.

paid price
    What you paid for a copy. Other collectors cannot see it. It is cleared
    when the card moves to a new owner in a trade.

parallel
    A version of the cards in a set, such as Base, Refractor or Gold
    Refractor. A parallel can have a print run.

Pond, Flock, Wetland
    Plan names in the sample Pricing Guide (pricing-guide.txt). That guide is
    fictional. The Application is currently free.

print run
    How many copies of a parallel were made, written with a slash, for
    example /50. A serial number cannot be higher than the print run. A
    variant can override its parallel's print run.

private want
    A want with Public unticked. Only you can see it, and it is not offered to
    other collectors for matching.

profit
    Sale price minus paid price, shown in the Sold report.

proposed
    A trade status. The offer has been sent and is waiting for the recipient.

PSA
    Professional Sports Authenticator, one of the four supported grading
    companies.

public want
    A want other collectors can see, so they can offer you a trade. Wants are
    public by default.

raw
    An ungraded card.

RC (rookie card)
    A card from a player's first year of cards. Marked with "Rookie card" when
    the card is added to the catalog.

Relic
    A variant that contains a piece of memorabilia, such as a piece of a
    game-worn jersey. See also: variant.

row-level security
    A database protection that lets each collector change only their own
    cards and wants, and see only trades they are part of.

serial number
    The individual number stamped on a numbered card, for example 23 of /50.
    Only one unsold copy of a variant can hold a given serial number across
    all collections.

serial-number conflict
    What happens when you record a serial number that another collector's
    unsold copy of the same variant already holds. Your card goes to an admin
    for review. See the Serial-Number Dispute Procedure.

serial-number review
    An admin's review of a serial-number conflict. You can follow it under "My
    serial-number reviews" in My collection. Statuses: pending admin review,
    approved, rejected.

set
    A product release, such as "2024 Topps Chrome (baseball)". A set has a
    sport, year, manufacturer and name.

SGC
    Sportscard Guaranty, one of the four supported grading companies.

similarity score
    A number shown with each search result, saying how close the passage is
    in meaning to your question. Results below 0.33 are not shown.

slab
    The sealed plastic case a grading company puts a graded card in. Its
    label shows the grade and cert number.

Sold report
    The report of your sold cards, newest first, with sale price and profit.

Summary report
    The report that totals your inventory, cards for sale and sold cards.

test user
    One of the shared sign-ins in the test release: Alice and Bob
    (collectors) and Carol (admin). Chosen with the Test user picker at the
    top of every page.

top loader
    A rigid plastic holder for protecting a raw card, usually used with a
    penny sleeve.

trade
    An offer between two collectors to exchange cards. A trade can be
    one-sided.

trade status
    Where a trade is in its life: proposed, accepted, declined, countered,
    cancelled or completed.

tradeable
    A setting on a copy. Only tradeable copies can be put in trades or appear
    in other collectors' matches. Turning it off cancels open offers that
    include the card.

Undo sale
    Reverses a sale entered by mistake. The card returns to your inventory,
    not tradeable.

variant
    A specific card in a specific parallel, optionally with an autograph or
    relic. Variants are what you add to your collection.

want
    An entry on your wishlist: a card or exact variant you are looking for,
    with optional minimum grade, maximum price and notes.

wishlist
    Your list of wants. The Wishlist page also shows matches in both
    directions.
