DUCKS ON THE POND CARDS
CAPABILITIES SUMMARY

Version: November 2026 (test harness release)

This document summarizes what the Ducks on the Pond Cards application can do
today, what it cannot do yet, and how it is built. For step-by-step
instructions see the User's Guide (users-guide.txt). For support terms see the
Customer Service Policy (customer-service-policy.txt).


1. AT A GLANCE

Ducks on the Pond Cards is a web application for sports card collectors. It
keeps a shared card catalog, tracks each collector's own cards, matches
collectors' wishlists against cards others are willing to trade, runs trades
from offer to completion, and reports on inventory, sales and profit.

  Available now:   catalog, collection tracking, grading and serial numbers,
                   tradeable and for-sale flags, sale records, wishlist with
                   matching, trades with counteroffers, reports, admin review
                   of serial-number conflicts, document search, health API.

  Not yet:         real sign-in, payments, shipping, card photos, market
                   price entry, email or push notifications, styled
                   interface, mobile app.


2. CATALOG

  - Shared by all collectors: anyone signed in can add to it.
  - Four levels: Set > Parallel > Card > Variant.
  - Sets record sport, year, manufacturer and name.
  - Every new set gets a Base parallel automatically; every new card gets a
    Base variant automatically.
  - Parallels can be numbered (a print run such as /50) or not numbered.
  - Cards record a card number (such as 150 or RC-12), one or more players,
    an optional title, and a rookie flag.
  - Variants can be marked Autograph and/or Relic, and can override the
    parallel's print run.
  - Duplicate entries are rejected.


3. COLLECTION TRACKING

  - Add a copy of any catalog variant to your collection.
  - Record per copy: serial number, grading company and grade, cert number,
    price paid, date acquired and condition notes.
  - Supported grading companies: PSA, BGS, SGC and CGC. Grades 1 to 10 in
    half steps. Ungraded ("raw") cards are supported.
  - Built-in checks:
      - A serial number cannot exceed the print run.
      - A grade needs a grading company, and a company needs a grade.
      - A cert number can be used only once per grading company.
      - You cannot record the same serial of the same variant twice.
  - Mark a copy tradeable or not tradeable.
  - Set a listed (asking) price.
  - Mark a copy sold with a sale price and date (defaults to today); undo a
    sale.
  - Delete a copy.


4. SERIAL-NUMBER PROTECTION

  - Across all collections, only one unsold copy of a variant can hold a
    given serial number.
  - If a collector adds a serial number someone else already holds, the copy
    is held for admin review instead of being rejected outright. The
    collector can attach a note for the admin.
  - Collectors can track their reviews (pending, approved or rejected, with
    the admin's note) on the My collection page.
  - Admins see both entries side by side and can change the existing copy's
    serial, delete it, then approve or reject the new one.


5. WISHLIST AND MATCHING

  - Want "any version" of a card, or one exact variant.
  - Optional conditions: minimum grade, a specific grading company, maximum
    price, and notes.
  - Wants are public by default (other collectors can see them) or private.
  - Automatic matching in both directions:
      - Other collectors' tradeable copies that meet your wants, each with a
        one-click link to propose a trade.
      - Your tradeable copies that meet other collectors' public wants.


6. TRADING

  - Propose a trade to any other collector, choosing cards to give and cards
    to receive from each side's tradeable copies. One-sided trades are
    allowed.
  - Attach a message to a trade.
  - The recipient can accept, decline or counter. A counteroffer starts with
    the original cards preselected and replaces the original offer.
  - The proposer can cancel while the trade is proposed or accepted.
  - Either collector can complete an accepted trade once the cards have been
    exchanged. Ownership of every card in the trade moves in a single step.
  - Safeguards:
      - Every card must be tradeable, unsold and owned by the side offering
        it.
      - Turning a card off as tradeable, or selling it, automatically cancels
        open offers that include it.
      - The same card can sit in several offers; the first to complete wins
        and the rest are blocked.
      - Cards that change hands arrive not tradeable, with the previous
        owner's paid and listed prices removed and today's acquired date.
  - Trade statuses: proposed, accepted, declined, countered, cancelled,
    completed.


7. REPORTS

  - Summary: inventory count, cost and market value; for-sale count and
    listed total; sold count, revenue and profit.
  - Inventory report: all unsold cards with paid, market and listed prices.
  - For-sale report: listed cards with margin (listed minus paid).
  - Sold report: sold cards, newest first, with profit (sold minus paid).
  - Reports show only the signed-in collector's own cards.


8. ADMINISTRATION

  - Admin role (Carol in the test environment).
  - Serial-number conflict queue showing the 50 most recent conflicts, open
    ones first.
  - Admins can edit or delete any copy for conflict resolution, and every
    decision records who resolved it and an optional note.
  - Admins can see all collection data. Only admins can manage market price
    data.


9. DOCUMENT SEARCH

  - Searches a corpus of reference documents by meaning (semantic search),
    not keyword matching.
  - Currently loaded with the Trailhead Supply Co. course documents
    (handbook, FAQ, operations manual, policies, pricing). It does not search
    card data.
  - Returns the top 3 passages with source, section and a similarity score.
  - Shows only passages scoring at least 0.33, and otherwise says "Not in the
    documents" with the best score found.
  - Questions up to 500 characters.
  - Searches are shareable by URL: /search?q=your+question.
  - Embeddings use the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model (384 dimensions), stored in
    Postgres with pgvector.
  - A developer command-line tool (npm run ask) can answer questions from the
    corpus using Claude; this is not yet part of the web application.


10. API

  GET  /api/health
       Reports whether the application and database are up.
       Responses: {"status":"ok","db":"ok"}, {"status":"ok",
       "db":"not_configured"}, or HTTP 503 with {"status":"error",
       "db":"unreachable"}.

  POST /api/search
       Body: {"query": "..."}. Returns {"query", "results": [{id, source,
       section, similarity, text}]}, top 3.
       Errors: HTTP 400 for a missing, empty or over-long query; HTTP 503 if
       search is not set up in the environment; HTTP 500 on failure.

Both endpoints are called from the browser the same way a future mobile app
will call them.

Collection, wishlist, trade and admin actions are performed through the web
pages' forms, not through a public API.


11. SECURITY AND PRIVACY

  - Row-level security in the database: each collector can change only their
    own cards and wants, and can see only trades they are part of.
  - Other collectors can see tradeable cards and public wants. The interface
    does not show them paid prices, listed prices, acquired dates, private
    wants or sold records.
  - Error messages shown to users never include database host or account
    details. Unexpected errors are logged as "action_failed" for follow-up.
  - Test login can be switched off per environment. When it is off, visitors
    can browse but not make changes.


12. PLATFORM AND TECHNOLOGY

  - Language and framework: TypeScript, Next.js (App Router), React.
  - Database: Postgres on Neon, with a separate development branch and
    production branch, and versioned schema migrations.
  - Hosting: Vercel, on Node.js 24.
  - Monitoring: structured server logs and Vercel Web Analytics (page views
    and visitors, once enabled in the dashboard).
  - Developer tools: seed data for development, corpus loading and query
    scripts, and a safety check that refuses to run development scripts
    against the production database.


13. CURRENT LIMITATIONS

  - Sign-in: test users only (Alice, Bob, Carol). There are no personal
    accounts or passwords yet.
  - Payments: none. Prices are records only; no money moves through the
    application.
  - Shipping: not handled. Collectors arrange delivery themselves.
  - Card photos: the database can store photo records, but uploading and
    viewing photos is not built yet.
  - Market prices: the reports can show them, but there is no screen for
    entering them yet, so the market column is usually blank.
  - Notifications: no emails or push notifications for trades or admin
    reviews. Collectors and admins must check the pages.
  - Editing: a copy's grade, serial or paid price cannot be edited after it
    is added (except by an admin changing a serial). Delete and re-add it
    instead.
  - Catalog: entries cannot be edited or deleted from the interface.
  - Interface: unstyled test harness; no mobile app yet.
  - Test data may be reset without notice.
